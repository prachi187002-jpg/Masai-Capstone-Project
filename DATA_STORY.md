# BigBasket Sales Performance: Data Story

## Overview

Performance is not uniform across categories. Some categories are exceeding targets and contributing positively to the business, while others are falling behind and require immediate attention.

## Category Performance

### Above Target
**Household Essentials, Personal Care, and Bakery** are all performing above their respective targets, indicating they are currently contributing positively to the business.

- **Household Essentials** is the strongest revenue contributor among the three.

### Watch Zone
**Dairy & Eggs** is below target but falls within the watch range. It requires attention now, before the gap becomes more serious.

### Critical
**Snacks & Beverages** and **Fruits & Vegetables** are in the critical category. Their performance needs immediate investigation.

## Key Insight

A single strategy will not work for every category. The categories above target, in the watch zone, and in critical condition each need a distinct approach.

## Recommendations

1. **Investigate the critical categories.** Conduct a product- and supplier-level analysis of Fruits & Vegetables and Snacks & Beverages to identify whether the issue is related to pricing, demand, availability, or supplier performance.

2. **Learn from the top performers.** Study the practices behind the strong performance of Household Essentials, Personal Care, and Bakery, and determine whether these successful approaches can be applied to the weaker categories.

## Conclusion

The dashboard shows that BigBasket has strong-performing categories, but future growth will depend on reducing the gaps in the underperforming ones.
