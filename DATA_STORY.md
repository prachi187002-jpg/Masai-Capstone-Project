# BigBasket Strategic Performance Story (Jan–Jun 2026)

## Executive Summary
Our **₹88,282 total revenue** hides a critical operational truth: BigBasket is overly dependent on high-margin stability while failing at daily-essential volume. With an **Average Order Value of ₹7,321** across only **434 orders**, our data indicates a low-frequency, high-ticket purchasing pattern rather than the high-frequency daily habit typical of successful e-grocery models. 

This friction is highly visible in our category splits. **Household Essentials (₹21,715), Personal Care (₹16,382), and Bakery (₹15,410) are ahead of target**, proving that consumers trust us for planned, non-perishable stock-ups and scheduled treats. Household Essentials anchors the business—outperforming Personal Care by **₹5,333**—because long-shelf-life items easily survive delivery friction.

## Strategic Gaps
Conversely, our fresh and immediate consumption categories are failing. **Dairy & Eggs (₹14,090) is below target but in the watch range**, trailing Bakery by **₹1,320**, while **Snacks & Beverages (₹10,895) and Fruits & Vegetables (₹9,790) are in the critical zone**. Fruits & Vegetables sits at the absolute bottom with a **₹11,925 deficit** compared to our top performer. For a grocery business, a critical failure in fresh produce suggests deep consumer distrust in our quality, freshness, or delivery speed.

## Category Team Interventions

### 1. Perishable Supplier & Cold-Chain Audit (Fruits & Vegetables)
Initiate an immediate supplier audit for **Fruits & Vegetables** and **Snacks & Beverages**. This underperformance signals chronic stockouts or poor cold-chain quality. We must re-evaluate vendor terms, enforce stricter transit SLAs, and correct these freshness bottlenecks to rebuild brand trust.

### 2. Operational Replay of Top-Performing Anchor (Household Essentials)
Institutionalize the operational blueprint of **Household Essentials** across our portfolio. We must study its inventory predictability and replicate those fulfillment standards to stabilize our weaker categories while expanding its catalog depth to maximize our return on marketing spend.
